    Open or install Platho

    Use it as an app, outside stores and platform gates.

    Platho installs as an app on this device. Once installed, the app itself, its guides and your recent encrypted history are kept here, so it opens faster and recovers better — but that is not a backup. It still needs the network to read the chain, update public feeds, fetch message history and send. No server account can read your messages or hold your keys.

      If your browser does not show the install sheet, use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

      Welcome to Platho. Your wallet is connected to the network - you can begin.

      Platho exists to secure a person's right to private correspondence, and is built so that this right depends on nobody's decision.

      What is available now

      • private conversations with any activated wallet
      • reading and publishing in the public feed
      • comments wherever the author left them open

      What to know before your first message

      Nothing written can be taken back. Messages and posts cannot be edited or deleted - they go into the blockchain and stay there forever.

      Public means public. Posts and comments are visible to everyone, both in the app and in the blockchain, in clear text. The author's wallet is visible too.

      Private stays private. Your correspondence is encrypted on your device: end-to-end, with post-quantum strength. Neither we nor the network hold the keys - only you and the person you write to can read it.

      Responsibility

      Weigh what you publish, especially in public threads. Every record in the blockchain has an author: a public post or comment may carry legal responsibility for them under the laws of their country, like any other publication.

      Welcome to Platho

      A keyless, end-to-end encrypted messenger on TON.

      Your keys live only on this device, and your messages travel through the TON chain encrypted — there is no server account that can read them or hold your keys. Private chats are end-to-end and post-quantum encrypted; everything is client-direct on TON, with no central gateway to block or censor.
